# November 2026 experiments

## Multi-Claim articles: the Source cap, the 8-Claim cap and false Claims in sound articles (2026-11-06)

`keep_shown_sources` fills the report's 12 Sources Claim by Claim, in order, and the Verdict
counts only those. Does that empty later Claims and change Verdicts on real articles? The
gold set cannot say: 93 of its 100 rows have one Claim. Bar fixed before measuring: the
effect is real if today's trim empties a Claim that had a firm Stance in at least 4 of the
23 articles, or changes at least 2 Analysis Verdicts.

**The set.** 23 real Spanish health articles, fetched on 2026-11-06 through
`extract_text_from_url`, so each text is what a URL submission sees, page chrome included,
cut at 10,000 characters: 14 from health news sites and 9 from wellness pages. They are
unlabelled and live outside the repo, next to the held-out set. Four candidates were
dropped because their site had closed.

```bash
uv run --directory backend --extra evaluation python -m evaluation.evaluate_pipeline --data <multi-Claim JSONL> --limit 46 --checkpoint results/multiclaim_trim_2026-11-06.jsonl
```

Git `dd5d382`, the production config (Mistral pay-as-you-go, `mistral-small-latest` in all
four roles), explanation off, live searches. A script then re-cut each row's
`claim_evidence` with today's trim and with a round-robin trim (every Claim's next Source
in turn) and decided both through `decide()`. Today's re-cut reproduced every recorded
Verdict.

|                                                    | Today's trim | Round-robin |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| articles where a Claim with a firm Stance loses it | **12/23**    | 7/23        |
| Claims emptied, of the 110 with a firm Stance      | 30           | 12          |
| Claim Verdicts that differ between the two         | 26           |             |
| Analysis Verdicts that differ                      | **0**        |             |

- **The bar is met on the first count and missed on the second.** Round-robin would fix
  Claim rows in the report but no headline Verdict, because two larger effects decide the
  Verdicts first.
- **The 8-Claim cap.** The extractor takes 13.2 Claims per article (304/23, at most 31);
  17 of 23 articles exceed 8, and 126 Claims (41%) are never searched. An unsearched Claim
  is uncertain, so under ADR-0003 an article with more than 8 Claims cannot be
  `verdadera`. None was.
- **Sound articles come out partly false more often than wellness pages:**

  |                 | `parcialmente falsa` | `incierta` | `verdadera` |
  | --------------- | -------------------- | ---------- | ----------- |
  | news sites (14) | 8                    | 6          | 0           |
  | wellness (9)    | 3                    | 6          | 0           |

- **Reading the 12 false Claims in those 8 news articles**, with the shown Sources that
  decided them (one reader, abstracts cut at 700 characters, no labels):

  | Cause                                                             | Claims |
  | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
  | contradicting Source asks another question, population or outcome | 7      |
  | an animal study taken as firm                                     | 1      |
  | a report of a study's result judged against the topic's consensus | 2      |
  | arguably right, on point                                          | 2      |

  In 7 of the 8 articles the `parcialmente falsa` rests only on Claims of the first three
  kinds. Examples: «la dieta mediterránea… reduce en un 30% la incidencia de diabetes»
  (PREDIMED) contradicted by PREDIMED's cardiovascular papers (HR 0.70 for CVD events);
  one drink a day shortening life, contradicted by a cohort on haemodialysis; «un estudio
  de la Universidad de Lund mostró que… no están vinculadas a un riesgo reducido de
  demencia», true as a report, contradicted by reviews of the diet's benefits.

- **9 of the 12 are study-specific**: a percentage, a subgroup or a named study's result.
  Retrieval answers them with Sources on the topic that test something else, the loose
  matching of 2026-10-15, here pointing against the Claim. On single-Claim gold rows it is
  rare; on articles it is the main failure.
- **One Source decides a Claim**: one `contradicts` and no `supports` gives a falsehood of
  0.67, over the 0.55 band, and one false Claim beside a true one makes the article
  `parcialmente falsa`. Three of the eight rest on a single Source.
- **The extractor distorts some Claims**: «una copa» became «un solo trago», and «7 hours'
  sleep plus healthy habits cuts the risk 65%» became a Claim about sleep alone.
- Latency: 20.6 s median and 43.9 s max per article, extractor to Verdict.

## Two contradicting Sources before a false Claim (2026-11-06) — rejected

Rule tested offline on the recorded Stances: a Claim is false only with at least two
`contradicts`, otherwise uncertain; the same applies where `_overall` falls back to the
band of the averaged falsehood, which an all-uncertain text reaches. It cannot create a
`verdadera`. Bar fixed before measuring: at least 4 of the 8 news articles leave
`parcialmente falsa`; gold recall on false drops by at most 3; gold accuracy on firm
Verdicts does not fall; negation pairs right drop by at most 2.

```bash
uv run --directory backend --extra evaluation python -m evaluation.evaluate_pipeline --partition gold --limit 100 --checkpoint results/gold_2026-11-06.jsonl
uv run --directory backend --extra evaluation python -m evaluation.evaluate_pipeline --partition negations --limit 30 --checkpoint results/negations_2026-11-06.jsonl
```

Git `7e56ff4` (a frontend-only change after `dd5d382`), same config, live searches.

|                                            | Bar      | Today                 | Two Sources   |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------- | --------------------- | ------------- |
| news articles leaving `parcialmente falsa` | ≥ 4 of 8 | —                     | 5 of 8        |
| gold: recall on false                      | drop ≤ 3 | 36/50                 | **22/50**     |
| gold: firm right                           | no fall  | 78/80 (97.5%, 91–99%) | 64/66 (97.0%) |
| negation pairs right                       | drop ≤ 2 | 26/30                 | 23/30         |

Wilson 95% interval in brackets.

- **All 14 gold rows the rule changes were right false Verdicts.** No false Claim resting
  on one `contradicts` was wrong there, so a count cannot tell a right single-Source
  Verdict from a wrong one. On the articles the problem is which Source contradicts, not
  how many.
- A first scoring missed the fallback in `_overall` and changed no gold row; a single-Claim
  text whose Claim turns uncertain is banded again on its own falsehood.
- **Today's baseline**: gold 78/80 firm right, recall on false 36/50, coverage 80%, one
  judge failure; negations 26/29 firm right (74–96%), 3 `verdadera→falsa`, against 30/30
  on 2026-10-14. Neither run replayed Evidence, so the comparison with October is not
  paired.

Open: the false Claims in sound articles. Most are study-specific Claims the literature
search cannot match, and the same extraction yields the 13 Claims per article behind the
8-Claim cap. The next lever is likely which Claims the extractor keeps from an article,
not the judge or the count rule; measure it on this set with a bar fixed beforehand.

## Extractor v6: the 8 central Claims of an article (2026-11-06) — not adopted

Three rules added to extractor v5, run from a copy of the prompts (`PROMPT_FILE_PATH`), so
the version is `v6-exp` and no prompt in the tree changed:

- no Claim repeated in other words;
- with more than 8 Claims, keep the 8 that carry the text's main health message (what it
  recommends, promises or warns about a food, treatment or habit), dropping definitions,
  context and history first;
- order the Claims from most to least central.

Bar fixed before measuring, on the 23 articles of the first 2026-11-06 entry: at most 3
articles over 8 Claims; at most 20 Claims unsearched; news articles `parcialmente falsa` at
least 3 fewer than the lower of two v5 runs; wellness articles flagged (`falsa` or
`parcialmente falsa`) down by at most 1. Then gold and negations as guardrails.

```bash
uv run --directory backend --extra evaluation python -m evaluation.evaluate_pipeline --data <multi-Claim JSONL> --limit 46 --checkpoint results/multiclaim_v5_rerun_2026-11-06.jsonl --replay-evidence results/multiclaim_trim_2026-11-06.jsonl
PROMPT_FILE_PATH=<prompts with extractor v6-exp> uv run --directory backend --extra evaluation python -m evaluation.evaluate_pipeline --data <multi-Claim JSONL> --limit 46 --checkpoint results/multiclaim_v6_2026-11-06.jsonl --replay-evidence results/multiclaim_trim_2026-11-06.jsonl
```

Git `3dfad0c` (docs only after `7e56ff4`), the production config.

|                                | v5         | v5 rerun   | v6        | Bar     |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ---------- | --------- | ------- |
| Claims (per article)           | 304 (13.2) | 298 (13.0) | 182 (7.9) |         |
| articles over 8 Claims         | 17         | 16         | **0**     | ≤ 3     |
| Claims never searched          | 126        | 122        | **0**     | ≤ 20    |
| news `parcialmente falsa` (14) | 8          | 5          | 4         | **≤ 2** |
| wellness flagged (9)           | 3          | 2          | 5         | ≥ 1     |
| searches replayed              | —          | 177/528    | 117/546   |         |
| latency, median                | 20.6 s     | 19.6 s     | 19.4 s    |         |

- **The bar fails on news articles**, so v6 is not adopted and the guardrail runs were not
  made. It removes the 8-Claim cap at no latency cost; what it does to Verdicts cannot be
  told from noise on this set.
- **The same config is not reproducible on articles.** The v5 rerun replayed only 177 of
  528 searches: on long texts the extractor on Mistral returns different Claims at
  temperature 0. 5 of 23 article Verdicts changed between the two v5 runs, 4 of them news
  articles. With that noise, 23 articles cannot detect a change of one or two Verdicts.
- **Searching every Claim does not make `verdadera` reachable.** Under v6 every article
  still has at least 2 uncertain Claims, with no firm Stance, so ADR-0003 keeps it from
  `verdadera`, as that ADR intends. The cap was the first obstacle, not the only one.
- v6 flagged 4 wellness pages the v5 runs did not, more movement than the 1 of 9 between
  the v5 runs. Without labels that is not a gain.

- **A fixed `random_seed` does not fix it.** Calling the extractor alone 3 times on each
  of the 4 longest articles gave 3 distinct outputs on 3 of them without a seed, and on
  all 4 with `random_seed=42`; the Claim count varied by up to 3 (8, 11 and 10 Claims).

Open: make a repeated run of the same article give the same Claims and Verdict, both for
users who reanalyse and so article sets can measure a change. The provider will not
repeat itself, so it has to be done in the pipeline or the harness. Then revisit v6 and the
false Claims in sound articles, whose lever is how the judge treats a Source on another
population or outcome.

## Gold misses by cause, and MedlinePlus for hoaxes (2026-11-11) — MedlinePlus rejected

Is the gold set's remaining error retrieval or the judge? Read offline from the 22 misses
of `results/gold_2026-11-06.jsonl` (git `7e56ff4`, the production config, live searches):
20 `incierta` and 2 `falsa→verdadera`. One reader, an AI assistant, working from each
row's queries, hit titles and kept Stances; abstracts were not read in full.

| Cause                                                | Rows | Examples                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| no paper tests it: a popular hoax or a textbook fact | 11   | microwaves make food radioactive, 10% of the brain, carrots and myopia, bleach, flu is influenza |
| literature exists, the query missed it               | 5    | masks and CO2, undetectable HIV load, biopsy needle-tract seeding, alcohol and cirrhosis         |
| the extractor dropped half the Claim                 | 1    | garlic «no sustituye a ningún tratamiento»                                                       |
| on-point Sources retrieved, judge or Verdict wrong   | 5    | hypertension headache and aluminium deodorants (`falsa→verdadera`), masks split 1–1              |

- **Retrieval is 17 of 22, and most of it is coverage, not ranking.** For the 11 no
  literature index answers them, keyword or embedding; reranking more candidates from the
  same APIs can only reach the 5.
- 12 of the 20 `incierta` are false Claims, consistent with the known ceiling of ~15
  unverifiable false Claims below.
- The alcohol-and-cirrhosis query carried the Spanish «cirrhosis hepatica» and over-long
  `AND`s; PubMed returned 0 hits. A translator or query bug, whatever retrieval comes next.
- openFDA returns unrelated drug labels (Naproxen, Mekinist, tamsulosin) for nearly every
  non-drug query, candidates the judge must read and drop.

**MedlinePlus as the source for the 11.** Bar fixed before querying: at least 6 of 11 with
an on-point page among the top 5 health topics of the MedlinePlus web service
(`wsearch.nlm.nih.gov`, `db=healthTopics`) means a reference corpus is worth building; 3 or
fewer rules MedlinePlus out. On point follows the judge's rule: the page states the
Claim's exact point, in either direction. The first query form, the English Claim, matched
almost nothing because the service requires every term, so before reading any result it
was switched to each row's recorded `search_query`.

| Result                 | Rows | Claims                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| on point               | 2    | flu is caused by influenza viruses; a biopsy examines tissue (read leniently)                                            |
| on topic, misleading   | 1    | Sudden Cardiac Arrest: the shock «can restore a normal rhythm to a heart that's stopped beating», for the asystole Claim |
| on topic, not on point | 1    | flu and cold are the same virus: only pneumonia and bronchitis pages                                                     |
| nothing returned       | 7    | microwave, 10% of the brain, garlic and COVID, bleach, carrots, both blood-donation Claims                               |

- **2 of 11, so MedlinePlus is ruled out.** Both hits are true textbook Claims; all 8
  false Claims miss. Lay health topics explain conditions; they do not refute popular
  beliefs, so a semantic index over them would not move the count past ~4 even if it
  found the Common Cold and blood-donation pages the keyword service missed.
- **A lay source can point the wrong way.** The defibrillator page simplifies to a
  sentence that backs the false asystole Claim; any reference corpus needs the judge's
  on-point rule as much as the literature does.

Open: the sources that do address these hoaxes are fact-checkers (ClaimReview via Google
Fact Check Tools, WHO myth-busters), which carries the in-sample risk stated in next
step 1 of the README; the held-out set has to exist before measuring one.
